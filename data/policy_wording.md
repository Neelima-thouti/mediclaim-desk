# Health Shield Policy Wording (synthetic sample)

## Definitions
Hospital means an institution with at least 15 inpatient beds that is registered with the local authority and has qualified nursing staff on duty round the clock. Medical necessity means that the treatment is required for the diagnosis or cure of an illness or injury and is not for convenience.

## Waiting periods
Initial waiting period: any illness first occurring within 30 days of the policy inception date is not covered, except injury caused by an accident. Specific waiting period: treatment for hernia, cataract and knee replacement is covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage for hernia and knee replacement, and 24 months for cataract. The waiting period counts from the original policy inception date, and continues across renewals without a break.

## Exclusions
The policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery unless needed after an accident or to treat cancer. Infertility treatment, self-inflicted injury, war and nuclear risks, and treatment taken outside a hospital are excluded. Dental treatment is covered only when it results from an accident and needs hospital admission.

## Room rent and associated charges
Room rent is covered up to 1 percent of the sum insured per day. If the insured chooses a room with higher rent, the claim is reduced in proportion for room charges and for professional fees linked to the room category. Consumables such as gloves, masks, disposable syringes and similar items are not payable.

## Co-payment
The insured bears 10 percent of every admissible claim amount. Co-payment is applied after all other deductions and before the sum insured limit.

## Claims procedure
Claims for reimbursement must reach the insurer within 30 days of discharge. The insured must send the duly filled claim form, the original hospital bill with itemised charges, the discharge summary, prescriptions and investigation reports, and a valid identity proof. The insurer may ask for further documents and may refuse payment for any document that is altered, incomplete or inconsistent.
